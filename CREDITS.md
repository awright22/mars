# Credits

Third-party work the game ships or is built from. A shorter list is shown in the game under
Title → Credits, which links to the full licence texts shipped with the game in `game/licenses.txt`
(generated by `tools/build.mjs` from the installed packages).

## Data

- **Mars elevation** — Mars Orbiter Laser Altimeter (MOLA) Mission Experiment Gridded Data
  Record, 32 pixels per degree (`megt90n000fb.img`). NASA / JPL / Goddard Space Flight Center,
  MOLA Science Team; distributed by the NASA Planetary Data System Geosciences Node. Public
  domain. Resampled and compressed by `tools/build_mars_data.py` into `game/assets/mars/height.bin`.
- **Mars colour** — Viking Mars Digital Image Mosaic 2.1 colourised global mosaic. NASA / JPL /
  USGS Astrogeology Science Center; tiles from NASA Mars Trek. Public domain. Reprojected into
  `game/assets/mars/color.jpg` and, with MOLA shaded relief, `game/assets/mars/map.jpg`.

- **Where the minerals lie** (Base mode) — no data files, but each kind of deposit is placed where
  it was really found, after these published detections: grey hematite at Meridiani Planum, Aram
  Chaos and in Valles Marineris (Christensen and others, 2000 and 2001, Thermal Emission
  Spectrometer on Mars Global Surveyor); opaline silica in Gusev crater (Squyres and others, 2008,
  the rover Spirit), at Nili Patera (Skok and others, 2010) and on the plains beside Valles
  Marineris (Milliken and others, 2008, CRISM on Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter); clays at Mawrth
  Vallis and Nili Fossae (Poulet and others, 2005, OMEGA on Mars Express), at Oxia Planum and in
  Eridania; layered sulfates in Valles Marineris and at Meridiani (Gendrin and others, 2005) and
  gypsum in Olympia Undae (Langevin and others, 2005), both OMEGA; chloride salts in the southern
  highlands (Osterloo and others, 2008, THEMIS on Mars Odyssey) and perchlorate on the northern
  plains (Hecht and others, 2009, the Phoenix lander). Positions are good to a degree or so, and
  the deposits themselves are the game's own.

## Models, sound and code

Every model in `game/assets/models/` is original work authored for this game by the scripts in
`tools/assets/` (no third-party meshes or textures). Sound is synthesised in the browser. The story, its
characters, its mission, its ships (the Tern) and its mission control (Control, a fictional international
programme) are the game's own.

## No endorsement, no insignia

The game is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by NASA, JPL, ESA, CNSA, Roscosmos or any other space
agency, or by the builders of any spacecraft in it. Real agencies appear only as the builders of the fourteen
historical landers, which are models made for this game and placed at their published landing sites.

Checked on 19 November 2026: no agency logo or insignia appears in any model, texture or icon. The models carry
only generic stencils (AIRLOCK 1, O2, H2O, OVEN, WATER, RATIONS, SUPPLY, N2H4 on a thruster tank) and, on the
Viking landers, the plain national flag the real landers carried. The icons are a rendering of the planet from
the elevation and colour data. No real astronaut's name or likeness appears anywhere: the astronaut's head (seen in the Hab with
the helmet off) is a generic low-detail head modelled for this game from simple shapes, not from any photograph or
person, and every person named in the game is fictional.
